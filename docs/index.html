Subscribable iCal feeds bundling national, regional and local holidays per city.

How subscribing works: pick a city and language below. "Subscribe" opens your calendar app directly; "raw .ics" is the plain URL if you'd rather paste it in yourself.

Either way, it adds a live subscription, not a one-time import — your calendar app re-checks the URL periodically, so you'll see updates automatically. In Google Calendar, use "Other calendars" → "From URL" and paste the raw .ics link. In Apple Calendar, use File → "New Calendar Subscription", or just click "Subscribe".
